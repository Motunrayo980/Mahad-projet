About Mahad Educational Consult Travels & Tours

Your trusted partner for study & work abroad, visa services, travel documentation and exam registration.

Who we are

Mahad Educational Consult Travels & Tours is a professional education and travel consulting firm dedicated to helping students and travelers achieve their global dreams. We specialize in study abroad programs, visa application support, exam registrations, and tourism services for destinations including the UK, Canada, USA, Schengen countries, and more.

In addition, we register and offer tutorials for international examinations such as IELTS, TOEFL, SAT, GRE, CELPIP, and GMAT, helping our clients achieve the scores they need for academic and immigration success.

  • Personalized admissions assistance and counselling
  • Visa application & documentation support
  • Exam registration and coaching
  • Tourist & vacation planning
